## Rights / production caveat

U.S. federal government works are commonly public domain when created by federal employees as part of official duties, but production use should still verify the exact credit and rights statement for each selected asset and confirm that no third-party rights apply. The live mockup is for proposal evaluation; final production should prioritize IAWF-owned photography, conference photography with releases, and approved partner/government assets.
